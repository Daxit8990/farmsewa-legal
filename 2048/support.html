Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Swipe up, down, left or right. Two tiles with the same number merge into one. Reach the 2048 tile to win.

Is my game saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. You can close the app and continue later.

How do I reset my scores?

Open Settings → Reset all progress.

Do I need internet?

No. The game works fully offline. Only these help and legal pages need internet.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
