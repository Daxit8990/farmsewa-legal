Privacy Policy

2048 · Last updated: October 4, 2026

Overview

2048 is a puzzle game made by FarmSewa. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your best scores, saved games and settings (sound, vibration, appearance) are stored only on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app uses vibration for game feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Third-party services

The app does not include third-party services that collect data. If you choose to tap "Contact us", "Rate the app" or "Share", your email app, the app store or the share sheet of your device opens, and their own privacy policies apply.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on this page, with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
